## Reduce Motion Behavior (verified by affordances tests)

When `reduceMotionEnabled = true`:
- Entrance: skipped — `entranceProgress.value = 1` immediately (no spring)
- Hold: skipped — `holdProgress` not updated with `withTiming`
- Press: still works (scale spring to 1)

When `reduceMotionEnabled = false`:
- Entrance: `withSpring(1, ENTRANCE_SPRING)`
- Hold: `withTiming(1, { duration: HOLD_FILL_MS })` over 3000ms
- Thresholds: stage ≥1/3 (1000ms), commit ≥2/3 (2000ms), push = 1 (3000ms)
